Our mission →

Founded on 13 December 2021, the Sathyabama Institute ACM Student Chapter is a growing community of students passionate about computing, innovation, and technology-driven problem solving.

As an active chapter under the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, we bring together learners who explore new technologies, build real-world projects, and contribute to the global computing ecosystem.

Our interests span software development, AI & ML, cyber security, data science, cloud technologies, and the application of computing in healthcare, education, business, and social impact.

As part of the Association for Computing Machinery, the world’s largest educational and scientific computing society, we draw on global resources, expert connections, and collaborative opportunities to support student learning.

A future shaped by innovation, technical excellence, and people.

Our vision is to inspire and empower students to become future-ready computing professionals through innovation, technical excellence, research thinking, and community-driven learning. We spark curiosity, enable collaboration, and help students discover the limitless possibilities of computing.

  1. Develop expertise in modern computing domains.
  2. Host hands-on workshops, expert lectures, hackathons, and coding competitions.
  3. Promote research thinking through papers and project development.
  4. Build a community where students collaborate, mentor, and grow together.
  5. Represent Sathyabama on national and international ACM platforms.
